USE YOUR OWN PHOTO
==================

Put any image here named exactly:

    custom.jpg

Then in the wallpaper settings set "Scene" to "My Photo".
That photo (the drone coastline shot you liked, a holiday picture,
anything) becomes the living background with weather on top.

Tips for the best look:
- Landscape orientation, high resolution (1920x1080 or larger).
- JPG or PNG both work (name it custom.jpg either way, or change the
  scene to "Custom URL" and paste a web link instead).
